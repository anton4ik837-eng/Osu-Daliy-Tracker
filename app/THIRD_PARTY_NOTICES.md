Sources:
https://github.com/kionell/osu-mania-stable
https://github.com/kionell/osu-classes
https://github.com/kionell/osu-parsers

The conversion engine is based on the current osu!lazer osu!mania ruleset port.
It should not be described as a byte-for-byte reproduction of osu!stable client output.
